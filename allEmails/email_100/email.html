Say hello to our new look
New Look, Same Kudos
Hi !

Get ready for an exciting new chapter! Kudos may look a little different now, but don’t worry - all the great features you know and love are still here.

What's new?

While you'll certainly notice our refreshed logo and vibrant new color scheme (not to mention our friendly Kudos mascot!), this transformation goes deeper. We've sharpened our product lineup to better align with our growth, values, and the ambitious future we have in store.

Why the change?

When we founded Kudos, we defined a clear goal for ourselves: make each purchase as delightful and rewarding as possible. We spotted an opportunity to streamline online shopping and help our members maximize their credit card rewards and perks. From there, we've developed a suite of tools that help you earn double rewards at over 15,000 stores with our free rewards program, find the perfect credit cards for your wallet, unlock card perks and benefits, and utilize our AI-powered credit card assistant, Maria GPT. We've come a long way in the last year and a half and this rebrand is our way of reflecting that growth. It's also a nod to where we started and where we’re headed next.

Renewing our commitment

This rebrand is more than a visual refresh; it's a reaffirmation of our dedication to our growing community of over 200,000 members. As we jump into this next chapter, the new Kudos is about growing together with you.
Thank you for your continued support and for being an integral part of Kudos. We’d love to hear your thoughts on our new look and are here to support you every step of the way. 

Reach out to us anytime.

Here's to a future as vibrant as our bold new colorway!
