Yellow! Same Kudos, New Look

In addition to our new funding announcement, we're rocking a new look. While you'll certainly notice our refreshed logo and vibrant new color scheme across all of our surfaces, the transformation goes deeper. 

Our design team wanted to reimagine what a financial company centered around people can look like. Our brand, created in partnership with Slope Agency, leads with a balance of friendliness and reliability. Our dog mascot got a massive makeover, but the core values it represents remain the same: after all, what’s more reliable than a man and woman’s best friend? Our friendly mascot is then paired with big uppercase letters, announcing our bold entrance into the world of credit cards.

While the traditional shades of blue that big financial institutions use remain a classic symbol of stability, we wanted to deviate from the status quo. The reality is that the majority of Americans don’t feel confident when it comes to their finances, and we need to position ourselves differently from what is currently available. Our new color palette leads with yellow to represent warmth, optimism, and friendliness, black to convey security and professionalism and utilizes purple as an accent color to help bring contrast into our visual language.

In addition to the logo change, our website, graphics, illustrations, and products have been revamped as well. Regardless of where our new designs live, our team will continue to rethink how we can deliver down-to-earth, user-friendly experiences to serve our diverse audience. Whether it’s to fetch you credit card points or guide you through your financial journey, we hope our designs will make your adventure as rewarding as possible.
